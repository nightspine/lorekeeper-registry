# Takedown policy

The registry holds index files: descriptions of characters, places and events, written in original wording by the people who share them. It never holds books or passages from books. If you believe a listing infringes your rights, this page says how to have it removed.

## Sending a notice

Email **akiraslingshot@gmail.com** with:

1. The listing (its address in this repository, or its publisher and title).
2. The work you hold rights in.
3. What in the listing you believe infringes, as specifically as you can.
4. A statement that you are the rights holder or are authorized to act for them, and that the information in your notice is accurate.
5. Your name and a way to reach you.

## What happens next

- Within two working days the listing is removed from lookup, so the app no longer offers it, and the person who shared it is told why.
- They may correct the listing and share it again, or reply with a counter-notice saying why they believe the removal was a mistake. A counter-notice is passed on to you.
- If you do not respond to a counter-notice within fourteen days, the listing may be restored.

## Repeat cases

A publisher with three upheld notices can no longer share to this registry.

## Public log

Every removal is recorded in `removed.json` with the listing, the date and the reason. Personal details from notices are not published.

## Other routes

This repository is hosted on GitHub, whose own [DMCA process](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/content-removal-policies/dmca-takedown-policy) also applies.
